Hearth Lodge Uploader

Hearth Lodge Uploader is a private tool used only by the Hearth Lodge team to upload and manage videos on the Hearth Lodge YouTube channel (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, schedules and playlists) through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
