Privacy Policy

Hearth Lodge Uploader · Last updated: November 7, 2026

What this app is

Hearth Lodge Uploader is a private tool run by the owner of the Hearth Lodge YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and update their details on that one channel. It is not a public service.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app can access the YouTube account they authorize, limited to what is needed to upload videos and manage their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, publishing schedules and playlists. The app does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other users.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish and manage videos on the Hearth Lodge channel.
  • The access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and is never shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Revoking it stops the app from accessing the account immediately.
